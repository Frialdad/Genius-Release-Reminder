# Privacy Policy – Genius Release Reminder

_Last updated: September 22, 2026_

Genius Release Reminder ("the extension") does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What is stored

When you save a release, the extension stores the following in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`:

- The page URL, title, artist name, cover image URL and release date of the song or album you saved.
- Your settings (language, theme, and whether and when to show the daily reminder notification).

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

## What is read

On `https://genius.com` pages only, the extension reads the page's own metadata (title, artist, cover and release date) so it can save the release when you click the bell button. It does not read any other website, your browsing history, or any account information.

## Notifications

If you turn on notifications in settings, the extension shows a local system notification at the time you choose with the releases you saved for the next day. Notifications are created on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Network requests

The extension makes no network requests of its own. Cover images shown in the calendar are loaded directly from Genius' image servers, the same way Genius displays them.

## Data retention and deletion

Saved releases stay in your browser until you remove them from the calendar or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data.
